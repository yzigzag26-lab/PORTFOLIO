04 — Manifesto

The Vision

“What foundational technology will Africa depend on tomorrow?”

Most people use technology. Very few build the foundations it runs on — identity, trust, verification, and infrastructure. My long-term direction is to learn those layers deeply and help build them: AI systems that are useful and accountable, cybersecurity thinking baked in from day one, blockchain and digital trust primitives, and automation that removes friction from everyday life.

TRUSTOS is my early exploration into that future — a trust-verification concept asking how digital information, credentials, ownership, and eventually AI agents could be verified in an open way. It is research, building, and experimentation — not a finished national-scale system. The ambition is long-term, and the work starts now.

Security FirstEthical-hacking mindset: understand systems, then protect them.
